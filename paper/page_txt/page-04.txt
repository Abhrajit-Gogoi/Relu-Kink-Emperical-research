Experimental Methodology

Dataset and setup. torchvision MNIST, pixels in [0, 1], no augmentation or standardization, cast
to the run dtype. Loss is cross-entropy; optimizer is vanilla SGD with learning rate 0.01, momentum
0, weight decay 0, batch size 128.
Initialization.
Weights drawn in FP32 with kaiming uniform (a=0, mode="fan in",
nonlinearity="relu"), then cast to the run√dtype; every
√ precision of a seed shares the underlying
draw. Biases, when enabled, drawn ∼ U(−1/ fan in, 1/ fan in).
Normalization. Explicit LayerNorm, RMSNorm, and BatchNorm1d reduce in the input dtype
(ε = 10−5 ), with keep native dtype=true. with bias and shift=true enables linear biases and
norm shift β, leaving scales learnable; false removes linear biases and sets β = 0. RMSNorm has no
shift; under false its learnable scale is removed as well.
Determinism. Python, NumPy, and PyTorch seeded; seeded DataLoader generator;
torch.use deterministic algorithms(True) where supported. A pair of s = 0 models sharing
θ0 and identical batches is the determinism control: their L1 distance must stay 0 at every step.
Custom operator. ProxyReLU is a torch.autograd.Function: forward max(0, x), backward
multiplies the upstream gradient by s only where the saved pre-activation compares equal to zero in
its native dtype, by 1 for x > 0, and by 0 for x < 0.
