Statistical power. With R = 5 paired seeds, the minimum mathematically possible two-sided
Wilcoxon signed-rank p-value is 2 · (1/2)5 = 0.0625, attained when one activation outperforms the
other on all 5/5 seeds. Thus p = 0.0625 indicates directional consistency across all seeds but is
underpowered to reject H0 at α = 0.05.
