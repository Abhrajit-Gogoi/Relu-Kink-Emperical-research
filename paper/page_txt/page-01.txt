Finite-Precision Proxy Derivatives at the ReLU Kink:
BFloat16 Arithmetic, LayerNorm versus RMSNorm, and Residual
Topology
Atharva Chavan, Abhrajit Gogoi
Independent Research Manuscript
November 2026

Abstract
Over the real numbers, the set of network parameters at which backpropagation through a ReLU
depends on the proxy derivative s = ReLU′ (0) ∈ {0, 0.5, 1} has Lebesgue measure zero. On
IEEE-754 grids, rounding and subtractive cancellation thicken that set into one of positive discrete
volume. We measure (a) how often single-step backpropagation lands in this non-differentiable
bifurcation zone and (b) how far coupled SGD trajectories diverge on a plain MLP, an on-highway
residual network, and an inside-branch Pre-Norm residual FFN block, across FP64, FP32, FP16,
and BF16, with LayerNorm versus RMSNorm, on MNIST with vanilla SGD (η = 0.01, batch size
128). Exact-zero collisions rise as the mantissa narrows: BF16 (p̂0 = 2.99 × 10−5 ) exceeds FP16
(3.80 × 10−6 ), which exceeds FP32 (2.54 × 10−9 ) and FP64 (0), despite BF16 sharing FP32’s
exponent range. LayerNorm concentrates pre-activation mass near zero relative to RMSNorm,
and mean subtraction keeps the exact-zero rate high even when additive bias is removed. An
inside-branch Pre-Norm residual branch whose skip path avoids the kink resists bifurcation far
longer than an on-highway residual ReLU. GELU (GELU′ (0) = 0.5) directionally outperforms
ReLU at s = 0.5 across all six precision × norm cells (lower loss total variation, higher test
accuracy), but with R = 5 paired seeds the minimum possible paired Wilcoxon p-value is 0.0625,
so H4 is underpowered to reject H0 at α = 0.05. The NeurIPS 2021 baseline reproduction does
not recover the published ∼35–45% FP32 bifurcation proportion (we measure 0.025 in Experiment
1 and 0.06 in the Stage-2 Monte Carlo cell); this discrepancy is documented rather than tuned
away.

Introduction

Automatic differentiation computes a valid Clarke subgradient at a ReLU kink only under restrictive
conditions. Park et al. [2024] (Theorem 1) show this holds when minibatch size B = 1 and all
non-differentiable neurons possess distinct bias parameters; Theorem 4 extends the result to arbitrary
minibatch size B ≥ 1 with distinct biases and a consistent convex-combination weight λl ∈ [0, 1],
which ReLU with s ∈ [0, 1] satisfies. Over the reals, the bifurcation zone—parameters where
backpropagation depends on s = ReLU′ (0)—has Lebesgue measure zero [Bertoin et al., 2021, Bolte
and Pauwels, 2021]. On discrete IEEE-754 floating-point grids, however, rounding and subtractive
cancellation create exact zeros z = 0.0, giving the bifurcation zone positive discrete volume. BF16
is of particular interest: it pairs FP32’s 8-bit exponent with only a 7-bit mantissa, so subtractive
cancellation can hit exact zero more often than FP16 despite FP16’s wider mantissa.

on/off, and three proxy slopes s ∈ {0, 0.5, 1}, using MNIST and vanilla SGD throughout. We test
