directionally outperforms ReLU at s = 0.5 across all six precision × norm cells (lower loss total
variation, higher test accuracy), but with R = 5 paired seeds the minimum possible two-sided
Wilcoxon p-value is 0.0625, so H4 is underpowered to reject H0 at α = 0.05.

7

Limitations and Future Work

The study is scoped to MNIST with vanilla SGD (η = 0.01, batch size 128) and fixed architecture
widths. The R = 5 paired-seed design imposes a hard statistical-power bound: the minimum possible
two-sided Wilcoxon p-value is 2 · (1/2)5 = 0.0625, above the α = 0.05 threshold, so directional
advantages cannot be confirmed as statistically significant. The 10-epoch horizon captures early
training stability but not long-horizon convergence. Extending Topology C’s inside-branch Pre-Norm
residual FFN to full multi-head self-attention Transformers, and evaluating additional activations
beyond ReLU and GELU, are left for future work.

8

Conclusion

Finite-precision arithmetic thickens the ReLU kink’s measure-zero bifurcation zone into one of
positive discrete volume. BF16 exhibits the highest exact-zero rate among the tested precisions
(2.99 × 10−5 ), exceeding FP16 (3.80 × 10−6 ), FP32 (2.54 × 10−9 ), and FP64 (0), despite sharing
FP32’s exponent range, because its 7-bit mantissa makes subtractive cancellation 23 = 8× coarser.
LayerNorm raises exact-zero collisions relative to RMSNorm through mean subtraction, an effect
that persists with bias off; both norms buffer test accuracy versus unnormalized networks. Topology
C’s inside-branch Pre-Norm residual FFN resists bifurcation far longer than Topology B’s on-highway
residual ReLU because its identity skip path bypasses the kink. GELU directionally outperforms
ReLU at s = 0.5 but the R = 5 design is underpowered (p ≥ 0.0625). The NeurIPS 2021 baseline
reproduction does not recover the published ∼35–45% FP32 bifurcation proportion (we measure
0.025 in Experiment 1 and 0.06 in Stage-2); this discrepancy is documented rather than tuned away.

Reproducibility Statement

All code, CSVs, tables, and figures live in the relu-kink/ repository. Every number in this
paper is read directly from the CSV logs under results/. Runners: run exp0 verify.py,
run exp1 monte carlo.py, run exp2 trajectory.py, run exp3 training.py; tables/figures are
regenerated by build tables and figures.py, which reads only the CSVs and fails on any
missing required column. Experiments use deterministic seeding (Python, NumPy, PyTorch), a
seeded DataLoader generator, and torch.use deterministic algorithms(True) where supported.
Weights are drawn in FP32 then cast, so every precision of a seed shares the underlying draw.
Experiment 2 was executed on a Google Colab T4 GPU; Experiments 1, 3, and 0 on an RTX 4060
laptop GPU. Bibliography entries and authors/years were checked against local PDFs.
