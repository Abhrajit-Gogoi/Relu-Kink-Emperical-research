Bias-on vs bias-off interaction. Disabling bias sharply reduces p̂0 in BF16/FP16 NoNorm and
RMSNorm—e.g., BF16 NoNorm drops from 2.99 × 10−5 (bias on) to 1.32 × 10−8 (bias off)—whereas
LayerNorm remains high (1.12 × 10−4 bias on vs 1.13 × 10−4 bias off) because mean subtraction
(xi − µ) generates exact zeros independently of additive bias. This supports H2: LayerNorm’s
centering, not merely the presence of bias, drives exact-zero collisions.
