Experiment 3 — multi-epoch training stability and GELU control

The topology-C residual MLP was trained 10 epochs on MNIST: precision ∈ {FP32, BF16}, norm
∈ {NoNorm, LayerNorm, RMSNorm}, activation ∈ {ReLU s = 0, 0.5, 1, GELU}, R = 5 paired
seeds, batch 128, η = 0.01. Output: per-epoch and per-run rows plus paired Wilcoxon tests against
s = 0. See Table 5.
GELU vs ReLU s = 0.5 (direct comparison, H4). GELU directionally outperforms ReLU at
s = 0.5 across all six precision × norm cells: strictly lower loss total variation and strictly higher final
test accuracy (Table 5). The paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test compares each activation against s = 0;
GELU vs. s = 0 gives p = 0.0625 for NoNorm/float32, LayerNorm/float32, LayerNorm/bfloat16,
RMSNorm/float32, and RMSNorm/bfloat16, but p = 0.2500 for NoNorm/bfloat16—the minimum
mathematically possible two-sided Wilcoxon p-value with R = 5 paired seeds is 2 · (1/2)5 = 0.0625,
attained when one activation wins on all 5/5 seeds. Direct GELU vs. ReLU(s = 0.5) comparisons
are not available from the experimental data; all Wilcoxon tests are versus the s = 0 baseline.
