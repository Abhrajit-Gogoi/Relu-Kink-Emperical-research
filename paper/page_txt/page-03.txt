LayerNorm computes x̂i = (xi − µ)/ σ 2 + ε with mean subtraction µ = d1 j xj . The subtraction
(xi − µ) can cancel to exact zero in finite precision, concentrating pre-activation mass at the kink.
RMSNorm computes x̄i = xi /RMS(x)—strictly positive scaling with no mean subtraction—so it
cannot generate zeros through centering. Both reduce in the input dtype (keep native dtype = True,
ε = 10−5 ).
